## publish

Submit extension to marketplace (tenant default; --public for global)

```sh
fmx publish [options]
```

- `--public`: Submit to global marketplace (requires platform scope)
- `--path <dir>`: Path to extension folder Default: `"."`.
- `--tenant <name>`: Tenant to authenticate with
